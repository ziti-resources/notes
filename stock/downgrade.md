# WARNING!!!
1. <b>[Anti-Rollback protection](./arb.md)</b>:- read this before doing anything
2. <b>Do not downgrade to OxygenOS 13 with unlocked bootloader</b>:- bootloader is broken in OxygenOS 13 for our device, if you downgrade to OxygenOS 13 with unlocked bootloader, it may brick your device
3. All internal data will be erased, backup your data before downgrading.

## [OxygenOS full OTA download links](./download.md)

# Steps:-
1. Turn on developer options:-
To enable developer option open settings > about phone > version > tap version no. 7-8 times.
2. Go to About Phone - Tap on OxygenOS.
3. Tap 3 dots at top and select "Local install" and select downgrade file.
4. Tap install 

Phone will reboot and all your data will be erased.

# FAQ
### Q. Local install option is greyed out
Ans. Turn off internet > Go to Settings > Apps > Tap on 3-dots on top right corner > Show system apps > Search updater > Clear data of updater and then repeat the steps with internet off.
